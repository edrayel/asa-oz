Copy Review — Messaging

Feedback says the current copy reads as too "mystic" / new-age. Below are three variants for the hero and intro sections. Choose one, or mix and match.

Hero headline + lede

Current (live)

Headline: Return to Self.

Lede: Asa-OZ is a cultural reconnection experience helping adults 45+ rediscover identity, belonging, and purpose through guided journeys, community, and meaningful travel.

Variant A — Grounded & concrete

Headline: Return to Self.

Lede: Asa-OZ is a community for adults 45+ who want to reconnect with their roots, meet like-minded people, and explore Ireland — and beyond — with purpose. We combine storytelling, shared meals, group conversations, and curated cultural journeys to help you feel at home again.

Variant B — Direct & explicit

Headline: Return to Self.

Lede: Asa-OZ helps adults 45+ rebuild a sense of identity and belonging through community, conversation, and cultural travel. This is not therapy, spiritual practice, or energy work — it is an invitation to share stories, meet people, and explore places that matter to you.

What-to-expect descriptions

Current (live)
  • Online events — regular sessions to connect, reflect, and grow together.
  • Group discussions & identity circles — safe spaces to share, listen, and be seen.
  • Cultural talks & storytelling — stories that remember you, and heritage that heals.
  • Hosted trips & curated journeys — travel that restores identity and awakens joy.
  • Community membership — a space to belong — details and fees to be confirmed.
Variant A — Grounded & concrete
  • Online gatherings — video calls, guest speakers, and group conversations from home.
  • Community meetups — in-person gatherings in Ireland for shared meals, walks, and storytelling.
  • Cultural sessions — guided visits, local hosts, and conversations about heritage and place.
  • Curated trips — small-group travel experiences in Ireland and eventually beyond.
  • Membership — ongoing access to the community, events, and trip announcements.
Variant B — Direct & explicit
  • Online events — video calls and group conversations for connection and reflection.
  • Community meetups — in-person gatherings in Ireland: shared meals, walks, and group discussions.
  • Cultural talks — storytelling and heritage sessions led by local hosts and guests.
  • Curated journeys — small-group cultural trips that explore identity through place.
  • Membership — details and fees to be confirmed. Join the waitlist for early access.

Tip: When you have chosen a direction, let your developer know and they can apply it site-wide. You can also add your own notes in the Feedback tab above.
