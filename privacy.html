Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This privacy notice explains how Asa-OZ collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website.

1. Who we are

Asa-OZ is a cultural identity restoration movement for adults 45+. For privacy questions, please contact us at info@asa-oz.com.

2. What information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name and email address when you join the waitlist or contact us
  • Your message when you use the contact form
  • Booking preferences when you request a discovery call

We do not collect payment information on this website. All enquiries are handled by email.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Add you to our waitlist and notify you when we launch
  • Arrange discovery calls and bookings
  • Improve our website and services

4. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to make the site work. With your consent, we may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors find us. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the cookie banner.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, email us at info@asa-oz.com.

6. Data security

Because this site is hosted as static files, form submissions are sent via your email client. We recommend using a secure email service. We do not store your information on web servers.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
