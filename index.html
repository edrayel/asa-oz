Identity • Culture • Belonging

Return to Self.

Asa-OZ is a cultural reconnection experience helping adults 45+ rediscover identity, belonging, and purpose through guided journeys, community, and meaningful travel.

Meet like-minded people who share your interests, values and curiosity. Whether you enjoy history, food, art, nature, photography or simply meaningful conversation, Asa-OZ helps you connect with others before travelling together.

What this is not. Asa-OZ is not spiritual practice, energy work, therapy, or meditation. It is a cultural and community experience — grounded in real conversation, shared meals, storytelling, and travel. If you are looking for healing, counselling, or spiritual guidance, this is not the right fit. If you want to connect with people, explore culture, and feel at home in the world — welcome.

No spam — only an invitation when we open our doors.

Thank you. You’ll be among the first to return to yourself.

Questions, gently answered.

What is Asa-OZ?

Asa-OZ is a cultural reconnection experience helping adults 45+ rediscover identity, belonging, and purpose through guided journeys, community, and meaningful travel.

Who is Asa-OZ for?

Adults 45+ who feel disconnected from their identity or heritage, are navigating a major life transition, long for meaningful community, want intentional travel experiences, or are seeking reflection and personal growth.

What do I receive after joining?

Online events, group identity circles, cultural talks and storytelling sessions, hosted trips and curated journeys, and community membership.

How long is the programme?

Programme length varies. Some experiences are one-off gatherings; others form part of an ongoing journey. Details will be shared as we finalise the calendar.

Is it online or in person?

Both. We begin with guided online sessions to build connection, then move into in-person community experiences and curated cultural journeys.

How much does it cost?

Membership fees are still being finalised. Join the waitlist to be the first to know when pricing is confirmed.

Why charge for the journey?

Our approach balances accessibility with commitment. We believe in building community first, and we design our programmes so that investment — whether free or paid — helps participants show up more fully. Paid programmes often signal value and encourage deeper engagement, while free community events ensure no one is excluded. The exact mix will evolve with your feedback.

Community first. Our initial circles and gatherings are free, because belonging should be accessible to everyone. Paid tiers come later, once the community is thriving.

Investment signals intention. Research and experience show that when people invest in a programme, they tend to engage more deeply and take it more seriously. We design our paid offerings to honour that commitment.

Which countries or cultures will be explored?

We are starting in Ireland, with plans to expand. Destinations will be announced as our community grows.

How often are experiences organised?

The calendar is still forming. We expect a mix of regular online sessions, monthly in-person circles, and periodic curated journeys. Exact cadence to be confirmed soon.

Is it UK/Ireland-based or international?

We are Ireland-based, with ambitions to grow internationally over time.

When do journeys take place?

Dates will be shared as journeys are confirmed. Join the waitlist and we will reach out with the first invitations.

Can I join from another country?

Yes. Many of our online sessions are open internationally. In-person journeys may be Ireland-based initially, but international participation is welcome where possible.

What happens after I register?

You will receive a welcome message with next steps, including invitations to upcoming online sessions and community events.

Is this therapy?

No. Asa-OZ is a guided cultural and personal development experience. It is not psychotherapy or a substitute for licensed mental-health care.

Do you sell travel packages?

No. Participants book their own travel. Asa-OZ provides cultural hosting and identity experiences — the grounding, the community, and the guidance that make the journey meaningful.
