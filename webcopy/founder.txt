My name is Ifeoma Adaora, and I’ve spent over 25 years of my life travelling the world, learning how deeply travel can heal and restore identity.
I created AsaOZ for adults 45+ who feel unseen, stuck, lonely, or unsure where to begin again. AsaOZ is a space for meaningful travel, cultural wellness, and emotional renewal. You don’t have to start over alone,
 I walk with you, guide you, and help you rediscover joy and purpose through travel.
FOUNDER STORY
I founded AsaOZ because I saw something happening quietly among adults 45+  especially those who have lived full, complex lives.
People were losing pieces of themselves.
Not dramatically.
Not loudly.
But slowly, quietly, over time.
Life transitions.
Migration.
Family changes.
Retirement.
Loss.
Loneliness.
The weight of responsibility.
I saw people who had spent decades caring for others
but had forgotten how to care for themselves.
I created AsaOZ to give them a place to return to themselves, through culture, community, belonging, and meaningful travel.

Not therapy.
Not wellness.
Not clinical intervention.
